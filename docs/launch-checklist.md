# Commercial Cleaning Business — Launch Checklist (Columbus, OH)

## Business setup
- [ ] Pick the business name and check it's available (Ohio Business Central)
- [ ] File LLC Articles of Organization with the Ohio Secretary of State ($99 filing fee; Ohio has no annual report)
- [ ] Choose a statutory agent (you can be your own if you have an Ohio street address)
- [ ] Get an EIN from the IRS (free)
- [ ] Open a business bank account
- [ ] Register with Ohio for withholding and unemployment if you hire W-2 employees
- [ ] Get general liability insurance, a janitorial bond, and workers' comp through the Ohio BWC (required once you have employees)
- [ ] Watch the Ohio Commercial Activity Tax threshold as revenue grows
- [ ] Buy a domain and email and point the website at it

## Website before launch
- [ ] Replace placeholders: "Your Cleaning Co.", (614) 555-0123, hello@yourcleaningco.com
- [ ] Remove "Insured & bonded" and "background-checked" until they're true
- [ ] Connect the quote form to a form service (e.g. Formspree) so requests reach your email

## First clients
- [ ] Set your pricing per visit
- [ ] Write a standard scope-of-work checklist and a simple service agreement
- [ ] Set up a Google Business Profile for local search
- [ ] Reach out directly to small offices, dental/medical practices and property managers
